Key Takeaways: Who: Local governments in Michigan. What: $37.5 million Drinking Water Asset Management (DWAM) grant introduced through the Michigan Clean Water Plan. Benefit: Helped communities manage their water infrastructure better, improved planning for assets, and provided funding for asset management software and improvements.
